Quick answers

Asked by travellers from Kochi.

How long is the flight from Kochi to Sri Lanka?

About 1 hr 10 min to Colombo. Short direct flights; the quickest international hop many Malayalis will ever take.

How much is a return flight from Kochi to Colombo?

Return economy fares typically run Rs 6,500 to 13,000, depending on season and how early you book.

Do I need a visa for Sri Lanka from Kochi?

Indian passport holders need an Electronic Travel Authorisation. It has been free for Indian tourists since 25 May 2026, valid 30 days with double entry. Apply only on eta.gov.lk.

Is Sri Lanka a good short trip from Kochi?

Yes. The flight is short enough for a long weekend: Colombo and Galle, or Kandy and the tea country.

Logistics

The port that moves India's cargo.

Colombo sits 6 to 10 nautical miles off the main east west shipping lane and handled a record 8.29 million TEU in 2025. A huge share of that is Indian transshipment. Adani runs the West Container Terminal. Mazagon Dock bought the island's biggest shipyard. Indian logistics money is already here. The question is whether yours is.

Talk logistics

Why Colombo matters to Indian cargo

Position

On the lane, not near it

Mainline vessels barely deviate to call Colombo. For Indian east and west coast cargo, it is the natural relay point between feeder and mothership.

Capacity

Deep water, new terminals

WCT and ECT add deep water berths for the largest vessels. When Red Sea and Gulf routings wobble, Colombo picks up volume.

Ownership

Indian skin in the game

Adani in the port, Mazagon Dock in the shipyard, IOC in fuel retail. Indian corporates did their diligence and wrote cheques. Follow the fleet.

Straight answers

Questions every Indian asks us.

Do Indians need a visa for Sri Lanka?

Apply online for an Electronic Travel Authorisation at the official eta.gov.lk before flying. Approval usually comes within hours. Sri Lanka has run free visa schemes for Indian passport holders, so check the current fee when you apply.

Can an Indian citizen buy property in Sri Lanka?

Yes, with rules. Foreigners cannot own freehold land directly, but you can buy condominium units above ground floor outright when the full price arrives as a foreign remittance. Land is held on long lease or through approved structures. From USD 100,000 invested in property you can apply for a renewable 5 year residence visa. Full details on our estates page.

Is my money safe? What about RBI rules?

Outward investment from India falls under RBI and FEMA rules, including the LRS limit for individuals. We tell every client the same thing: structure it with your CA in India and a licensed lawyer in Colombo. We introduce you to both. Nothing on this site is investment advice.

What is this 0% and 7.5% tax talk about Port City?

Port City Colombo is a special economic zone with its own rulebook. Approved primary businesses receive corporate tax holidays of 8 to 15 years, secondary businesses 7.5% for 4 years, and personal income tax inside the zone has been 0%. Every incentive needs approval from the Colombo Port City Economic Commission. Our enterprise page sets it out.

Is Sri Lanka stable enough to invest in?

A fair question. The country defaulted in 2022, restructured, and has been rebuilding under an IMF program since. Ratings have improved and tourism touched records in early 2026. It remains an emerging market with emerging market risk. That is precisely why prices are attractive, and why you enter with good local partners rather than alone.

Will language be a problem?

No. English carries business and tourism. Tamil is an official language. Hindi earns smiles and often a Bollywood conversation. Menus, contracts and lawyers all work in English.

Trade in rupees

Invoice in rupees. Skip the dollar.

Indian and Sri Lankan businesses can invoice, pay and settle trade in Indian rupees. It runs through RBI's Special Rupee Vostro Accounts, and Sri Lanka lists the rupee among its designated foreign currencies. No dollar conversion at either end, less currency risk, and books kept in the currency you already report in.

  • ₹ invoicesExports and imports priced, paid and settled in INR.
  • ₹ creditSince October 2025, Indian banks can lend rupees to Sri Lankan businesses and banks for cross border trade.
  • Faster setupSince July 2026, Indian banks open rupee vostro accounts for overseas banks without a separate RBI approval.

Straight answers

Questions every Indian asks us.

Do Indians need a visa for Sri Lanka?

Indian passport holders apply online for an Electronic Travel Authorisation before flying. Since 25 May 2026 the 30 day tourist ETA, with double entry, is free for Indians. Apply only on the official eta.gov.lk portal.

Can an Indian buy property in Sri Lanka?

Foreigners cannot own freehold land, but can buy condominium units on any floor outright when paid through an inward foreign remittance. Land is typically held on long lease. An investment of USD 100,000, including a condo purchase, supports a 5 year investor visa, and USD 200,000 a 10 year one.

What is the tax rate in Port City Colombo?

Under Regulation 01 of 2025, primary Businesses of Strategic Importance get corporate income tax holidays of 8 to 15 years, secondary businesses a 7.5% concessionary rate for 4 years, and all incentives need approval. Since 20 January 2026, staff of newly approved Port City companies pay normal income tax; earlier approvals keep a staff exemption until January 2029.

How much does a destination wedding in Sri Lanka cost compared to India?

Sri Lankan beach resorts often price venue, rooms and catering below comparable Goa or Udaipur packages, while international flights narrow the gap depending on your city. Run your own numbers in our calculator and ask us for three real resort quotes.

Can Indian businesses trade with Sri Lanka in rupees?

Yes. India and Sri Lanka trade can be invoiced, paid and settled in Indian rupees through Special Rupee Vostro Accounts that Sri Lankan banks hold with Indian banks. Since October 2025, Indian banks can also lend rupees to Sri Lankan businesses for cross border trade.

How far is Sri Lanka from India?

Colombo is about 1 hour 20 minutes by air from Chennai, around 2 hours from Bengaluru and Hyderabad, and about 2 hours 30 minutes from Mumbai. A passenger ferry also runs between Nagapattinam and Kankesanthurai near Jaffna.

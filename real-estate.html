Can Indians buy property in Sri Lanka? Yes, with two rules.

Indians can buy apartments (condominium units) outright, on any floor, and lease land for up to 99 years. You cannot own freehold land. The full price must come in from abroad through an Inward Investment Account before the deed is signed. Spend USD 100,000 and the purchase can support a 5 year investor visa.

Can Indians buy land in Sri Lanka?

Not freehold. Since the 2014 Act, any transfer of freehold land to a foreigner is void. The working route is a long lease of up to 99 years: villa buyers on the south coast lease the land, then build or renovate. Leased land held by a foreigner cannot be mortgaged for five years from the lease date.

The India side: LRS, TCS and your ITR

LRS

USD 250,000 per person, per year

RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme allows buying property abroad. Resident family members can pool their limits for one purchase.

TCS

20% above ₹10 lakh

Banks collect tax at source on property remittances above ₹10 lakh a year in the 2026 to 27 financial year. It is a credit you claim back against your income tax, not a cost, but plan the cash flow.

ITR

Report it in Schedule FA

Resident taxpayers must declare foreign property every year. Skipping it risks a ₹10 lakh penalty under the Black Money Act.

Rent

Keep it there or bring it home

Rent from an LRS investment can be reinvested abroad. Money you do not use or reinvest comes back to India within 180 days. On the Sri Lankan side, rent and sale proceeds return to your Inward Investment Account and can be sent out.

The investor visa: USD 100,000 for 5 years

Sri Lanka's investor visa gives a 5 year residence visa for USD 100,000 and 10 years for USD 200,000, with your spouse and dependants included. Buying a condo counts as an eligible investment, with the money routed through an Inward Investment Account. The visa fee is USD 200 per person per year. Quick answers
Can Indians buy property in Sri Lanka?

Yes. Indians can buy condominium units outright on any floor, paid in full from abroad through an Inward Investment Account. Freehold land is barred for foreigners, but land can be leased for up to 99 years.

Can Indians buy land in Sri Lanka?

Not freehold. The Land (Restrictions on Alienation) Act No. 38 of 2014 bars transfers of freehold land to foreigners. Indians can lease land for up to 99 years and build on it.

Does buying property in Sri Lanka give residency?

It can. An eligible investment of USD 100,000, including a condo purchase routed through an Inward Investment Account, supports a 5 year investor visa. USD 200,000 supports 10 years.

How much money can I send from India to buy property abroad?

Up to USD 250,000 per person per financial year under RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme, and family members can pool their limits. Banks collect 20% TCS on amounts above Rs 10 lakh a year, which you claim back against your income tax.

What taxes do you pay when buying property in Sri Lanka?

Stamp duty of 3% on the first LKR 100,000 and 4% on the rest, 18% VAT on new condos from VAT registered developers, and legal fees of around 1% to 3%.

Can I rent out my Sri Lankan apartment and bring the money to India?

Yes. Rent goes into your Inward Investment Account and can be sent abroad. Under RBI rules you can reinvest it, or bring unused funds back to India within 180 days.

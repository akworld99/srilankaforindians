Second Destination

A second address, 400 km from home.

Dubai is 3,000 km away and had a complicated year. Singapore is expensive. Sri Lanka is an hour from Chennai, runs on English, takes UPI, and lets you build a base without leaving your parents a long flight behind. This is the quiet option smart families are taking seriously.

Plan your move

Ways to stay long term

Property route

Investor residence visa

USD 100,000 or more into qualifying property supports a renewable 5 year residence visa. Buy the apartment, get the base.

Business route

Resident visa via your company

Directors and key staff of registered businesses, including Port City entities, qualify for employment and investor visas.

Flexible route

Long stays without committing

Tourist ETAs with extensions let you trial island life for months before you buy anything. Try one monsoon first. We mean it.

The honest trade offs

You gain

Time, air and ocean

Shorter commutes, cleaner air than most metros, beaches 20 minutes from the office, and a pace that lets families actually see each other. Plus a hedge: assets and options outside one country's system, still an hour from home.

You accept

Emerging market life

Imported goods cost more. Bureaucracy takes patience. The economy is recovering from a hard crisis and policies can shift. This works for people who plan properly, which is why you talk to us first.

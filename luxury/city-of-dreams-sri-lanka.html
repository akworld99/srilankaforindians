City of Dreams Sri Lanka

City of Dreams, explained for Indians.

South Asia's first integrated resort opened in Colombo in August 2025: two hotels, a casino, a mall, a spa and 17 places to eat and drink, built by John Keells and Macau's Melco. Here is what is inside and how to do it well.

Where to eat and drink

  • Indiya: the Indian restaurant, for when the group wants dal and tandoor.
  • Sapphire Dragon (Cantonese), Yoroko (Japanese), Bistro des Marées and Staten Bar & Grill.
  • Gatz: a two storey champagne bar and supper club with live bands every night.
  • Flux (rooftop lounge), Dreams & Beats (rooftop pool bar) and Pool + Bar 09.

Shows and big nights

Hrithik Roshan performed at the opening weekend in August 2025. Mercedes-Benz Fashion Week Sri Lanka ran at Cinnamon Life in October 2026, and This Is Michael, a Michael Jackson tribute, plays The Podium on 8 and 9 November 2026. See the full events calendar.

Casino basics

  • Expect a minimum age of 21, a passport check at the door, and a smart casual dress code: no sleeveless shirts, shorts or slippers.
  • Sri Lankan citizens pay a USD 100 entry levy. Ask at the door about any charge for foreign passport holders; reports have differed.
  • Sri Lanka's new Gambling Regulatory Authority came into force on 1 December 2025, with detailed regulations still being finalised in 2026.

What a night costs

Rooms at Cinnamon Life were booking at around ₹14,600 to 17,800 a night in October 2026. Nüwa suites cost considerably more; we quote on request. Weekends and the Christmas to New Year fortnight run highest.

Getting there

City of Dreams is on the beachfront in Colombo 2, next to the Beira Lake and a short drive from Galle Face. Fly into Colombo, 80 minutes from Chennai, about 90 minutes from Bengaluru and two and a half hours from Mumbai.

Casinos in Sri Lanka are for adults only. Set a limit before you go in and treat it as entertainment. Indian forex cards and LRS remittances cannot be used for gambling, so we do not arrange gaming funds, credit or junkets.

Quick answers
What is City of Dreams Sri Lanka?

South Asia's first integrated resort, in Colombo, built by John Keells and Macau's Melco. It combines the Cinnamon Life hotel (687 rooms), the Nüwa hotel (113 rooms), a casino, a luxury mall, a spa, ballrooms and about 17 restaurants and bars.

When did City of Dreams Sri Lanka open?

Cinnamon Life opened in October 2024. The casino and the Nüwa hotel opened with the resort's grand opening on 1 and 2 August 2025.

Is there an Indian restaurant at City of Dreams Colombo?

Yes. Indiya is the resort's Indian restaurant, alongside Cantonese, Japanese and European dining.

What are the casino entry rules at City of Dreams Sri Lanka?

Expect a minimum age of 21, a passport check and a smart casual dress code. Sri Lankan citizens pay a USD 100 entry levy; ask at the door about any charge for foreign visitors.

How much does a room at City of Dreams Colombo cost?

Cinnamon Life rooms were booking at around Rs 14,600 to 17,800 a night in October 2026. Nüwa suites cost more and are quoted on request.

Mirissa, Weligama, Ahangama, Hiriketiya

The south coast, after dark.

For a lot of Indian groups, New Year's Eve means the south coast, not a city ballroom. December is dry season down there, the surf towns throw the parties, and Galle is two hours from the airport.

Getting there

  • Colombo airport to Galle: about 2 hours by car, just over 2 to Ahangama, about 3 to Tangalle.
  • Colombo to Galle: about 1 hour on the Southern Expressway from Kottawa, or 2 to 3 hours on the coastal train.
  • Galle to Mirissa: 35 to 60 minutes by car.
  • In a hurry: helicopter transfers from the airport to Galle take about an hour.

Rules that matter down south

  • Drugs: do not. Sri Lanka has some of the harshest drug laws in Asia. Holding 5 grams or more of pure heroin, cocaine or methamphetamine carries death or life imprisonment, a life sentence was handed down for about 2 grams of heroin in 2025, and cannabis brings long prison terms. Police made more than 1.6 lakh drug arrests in a national operation between October 2025 and April 2026.
  • Noise: outdoor loudspeakers are restricted late at night unless an event holds a police licence, so the big nights happen at licensed venues and hotels. Buy tickets from the venue itself.
  • The sea: December to April is the south coast's calm, dry season, but currents still catch swimmers. Swim at lifeguarded beaches; Mirissa's police life saving unit rescued a tourist in December 2025.
  • Poya: Wednesday 23 December 2026 is Unduvap Poya. Bars and liquor shops close.
  • Age: 21 to drink.

Come for the surf too

The south coast is in surf season from about November to March. Weligama is the beginner's bay, Ahangama has breaks from Kabalana to Midigama, and Hikkaduwa hosted Red Bull Ride My Wave in 2025.

Quick answers
Where is the best New Year's Eve party in Sri Lanka?

On the south coast, Hiriketiya's Dots Bay House sold out its New Year's Eve 2025 party, and resorts like Anantara Peace Haven Tangalle, Amangalla and Cape Weligama host galas with fireworks. In Colombo, the big public party is at Galle Face Green.

How much is New Year's Eve at Anantara Tangalle?

The Celestial Gala on 31 December 2026 costs USD 390 per adult and USD 180 per child, taxes included, with a buffet, fireworks and an after party.

Is Mirissa good for New Year's Eve?

Yes. December is the dry season on the south coast, Mirissa's beach bars run party nights, and Weligama, Ahangama and Hiriketiya are a short drive away.

How far is the south coast from Colombo airport?

About 2 hours to Galle, just over 2 hours to Ahangama and about 3 hours to Tangalle by car.

What are Sri Lanka's drug laws for tourists?

Extremely strict. Possession of 5 grams or more of pure heroin, cocaine or methamphetamine carries death or life imprisonment, and cannabis brings long prison sentences. Do not use or carry drugs.

Bachelor and bachelorette

The last weekend, done properly.

Goa is crowded and Bangkok is four hours away. Colombo is 80 minutes from Chennai, the visa is free, and the whole weekend fits into five star hotels, a boat and three very good rooftops.

Plan it with us

A two night plan that works

WhenPlan
Friday afternoonLand in Colombo, check in at City of Dreams, The Kingsbury or a Galle Face hotel. Pool time
Friday sunsetRooftop drinks at ON14 or Flux at Cinnamon Life
Friday nightDinner, then live music at Gatz supper club or DJs at SunsetBlu
Saturday dayA crewed catamaran sail, or a day in Bentota for jet skis, water skiing and donut rides on the river
Saturday nightThe big night: a reserved table at Sky Lounge or Flux, with the group's driver on standby
SundayLate brunch, Tiki Bar at the Shangri-La for one last view of Galle Face, fly home

The south coast version

Swap Colombo for the surf towns: a villa in Ahangama or Weligama, Wednesday night at The Doctor's House near Mirissa, Saturday at Dots Bay House in Hiriketiya, and cocktails at Smoke & Bitters or Raa. Galle is about two hours from the airport. See south coast parties.

On the water

Sail Lanka Charter runs crewed catamarans from Colombo, Mirissa and Trincomalee, by the hour, day or week, including sunset sails. Port City's new marina is still being built and is due in 2027, so confirm the departure point when you book.

For the bachelorette

  • Spa morning at your hotel, then a long lunch by the pool.
  • Shopping at One Galle Face and The Mall at Port City.
  • Sundowners at Vistas or Level 4, dinner, then the supper club.
  • A south coast extension: two nights in a Galle or Tangalle villa, and Smoke & Bitters at Dikwella.

Group logistics

  • Visa: the tourist ETA is free for Indians. Each person applies on eta.gov.lk.
  • Dates: avoid Poya days, when bars close. Next: 24 November and 23 December 2026, 22 January and 20 February 2027.
  • Age: everyone needs to be 21 to drink.
  • Getting around: one van and driver for the whole weekend beats six cabs.
  • Casino: City of Dreams has one. It is optional, adults only, and a night out is not a strategy. Agree a limit as a group.

Casinos in Sri Lanka are for adults only. Set a limit before you go in and treat it as entertainment. Indian forex cards and LRS remittances cannot be used for gambling, so we do not arrange gaming funds, credit or junkets.

Quick answers
Is Colombo good for a bachelor party from India?

Yes for a short, high end weekend: two nights of rooftops, a supper club, a boat or Bentota water sports day, and five star hotels, 80 minutes from Chennai with a free visa.

How many days do you need for a bachelor weekend in Colombo?

Two nights covers it. Add two nights on the south coast for a longer trip.

Can we hire a yacht in Colombo?

Crewed catamaran sails run from Colombo, Mirissa and Trincomalee. Port City's marina is due in 2027, so confirm the departure point when you book.

What should groups avoid when planning?

Poya days, when bars close, and arriving without a driver for the group. Book tables at the rooftop bars ahead on Friday and Saturday.

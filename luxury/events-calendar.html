Cricket fans, note

There is no men's international cricket in Sri Lanka in this window. Sri Lanka tours India for three ODIs and three T20Is from 13 to 27 December 2026, then New Zealand from mid January. The Lanka Premier League ran in July and August 2026.

Plan around the Poya

Full moon Poya days are public holidays when bars close. They are also some of the most beautiful nights to be in Sri Lanka, with lanterns and temple processions. Book the party night either side.

Quick answers
What events are on in Colombo in December 2026?

Local concerts on 4, 5 and 11 December, Unduvap Poya on 23 December, Christmas on 25 December and New Year's Eve on 31 December, when Colombo has had fireworks over Galle Face Green and Port City.

When is Deepavali in Sri Lanka in 2026?

Sunday 8 November 2026, a public holiday in Sri Lanka.

Is there cricket in Sri Lanka between November 2026 and March 2027?

No men's internationals are scheduled in Sri Lanka in that window. Australia women are due to play three ODIs in Sri Lanka in December 2026.

When is the Navam Perahera in 2027?

The date is yet to be announced. It is held by Gangaramaya temple around Navam Poya; in 2026 it ran on 31 January and 1 February.

Luxury weekend

Two nights in Colombo, done well.

Suites over the Indian Ocean, a crab dinner people fly in for, a private yacht at sunset and a supper club after. Closer than Dubai, cheaper than the Maldives, and on Indian time.

Where to eat

  • Ministry of Crab, Old Dutch Hospital: Colombo's most famous table, on Asia's 50 Best Restaurants extended list. Book ahead.
  • Nihonbashi, now at Port City: the Japanese institution, 30 years old in 2025, with eight private dining rooms.
  • Indiya, Sapphire Dragon and Yoroko at City of Dreams.

What to do

  • Port City: walk the 500 metre Marina Promenade and swim at the free beach. The superyacht marina is due in 2027.
  • On the water: Browns' private yacht Saakya takes up to 15 guests from Port City; sunset catamaran sails also run from Colombo.
  • Spa: Kurundu Spa at City of Dreams, or your hotel's.
  • Shopping: The Shoppes at City of Dreams, One Galle Face and The Mall at Port City.
  • Nights out: rooftops, the supper club and live music. See Colombo nightlife.

Quick answers
What are the best luxury hotels in Colombo?

Nüwa and Cinnamon Life at City of Dreams, ITC Ratnadipa, Shangri-La Colombo, the Galle Face Hotel and Cinnamon Grand are the top tier in 2026.

Is there an Indian hotel brand in Colombo?

Yes. ITC Hotels opened ITC Ratnadipa in April 2024, with 352 rooms, nine restaurants and bars and a helipad. Cinnamon Grand opened in 1975 as the Lanka Oberoi.

Can you hire a yacht in Colombo?

Yes. Browns' private yacht Saakya takes up to 15 guests from Port City, and crewed catamaran sunset sails run from Colombo. Port City's superyacht marina is due in 2027.

How much does a luxury weekend in Colombo cost from India?

Rooms at Cinnamon Life ran about Rs 14,600 to 17,800 a night and ITC Ratnadipa about USD 205 to 270 in October 2026, before flights, dining and experiences. Suites and the festive season cost more.

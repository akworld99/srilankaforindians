Sri Lanka in December 2026: weather, where to go, what is on

December opens peak season on Sri Lanka's west and south coasts, and Indians fill it: 56,715 arrived in December 2025 alone. The trick is knowing which side of the island to be on, because the northeast monsoon is soaking the other one.

What is on in December 2026

  • Unduvap Poya, Wednesday 23 December: a public holiday, and traditionally the start of the Adam's Peak (Sri Pada) pilgrimage season, which runs to Vesak in May. Liquor shops close on Poya days; star hotels usually serve.
  • Christmas, Friday 25 December: a public holiday. Colombo's malls and hotels decorate from late November.
  • New Year's Eve: fireworks over Galle Face Green and Port City. See our New Year in Sri Lanka guide.
  • Whale watching: Mirissa's season runs through the northeast monsoon months, from about late November to April.
  • Leopards: Yala is open. Blocks 1 and 2 closed for drought from 14 September and reopened on 16 October 2026.

What to skip in December

  • The east coast beaches of Trincomalee, Pasikudah and Arugam Bay. Save them for May to September.
  • The Minneriya elephant gathering, which happens from about July to October.
  • The Nagapattinam ferry, paused for the monsoon. Fly to Colombo or Jaffna instead.
  • A through train from Kandy to Ella. Repairs after Cyclone Ditwah are due to finish around the end of 2026 or early 2027. Board at Nanu Oya instead; that section is running.

Book early

The Christmas and New Year fortnight lines up with Indian school winter breaks, and it is the most expensive time of the season. Lock flights and south coast hotels by October or early November. Quick answers
Is December a good time to visit Sri Lanka?

Yes, for the west and south coasts and the hill country. December opens peak season on the south coast with clear skies and calmer seas. The east coast and the north get northeast monsoon rain, so skip those beaches.

Which part of Sri Lanka is best in December?

The south coast (Galle, Mirissa, Tangalle), Colombo and the hill country (Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Ella). Yala is open for safaris and Mirissa's whale season is under way.

Is it cold in Sri Lanka in December?

Only in the hills. Colombo stays around 31 degrees by day, but Nuwara Eliya averages about 19 degrees by day and 11 at night, so pack a warm layer.

Can you climb Adam's Peak in December?

The pilgrimage season traditionally opens on Unduvap Poya, which falls on 23 December 2026, and runs to Vesak in May. Before that, the climb is off season.

Is the Kandy to Ella train running in December 2026?

Not end to end. The Nanu Oya to Ella section is running, but sections from Kandy are still being repaired, with completion expected around the end of 2026 or early 2027.

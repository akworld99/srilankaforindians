How to set up a company in Sri Lanka from India, 2026

An Indian founder can own a Sri Lankan private company outright in most sectors, register it online in about two weeks, and run it with a single director. Here is the full path, from name approval to the India side paperwork.

The steps

  1. Reserve the name on the Registrar of Companies' e-ROC portal. Usually 3 to 5 days. Fee LKR 2,600 plus VAT.
  2. File incorporation: Forms 1, 18 and 19 with the articles of association. The certificate usually follows in 5 to 12 days. Fee LKR 5,200 plus VAT for a private company.
  3. File beneficial ownership: since 30 March 2026, incorporation is only complete once the company declares who ultimately owns it.
  4. Open an Inward Investment Account at a Sri Lankan bank. Foreign share capital must come in through it, and dividends and sale proceeds go back out through the same account.
  5. Register for tax with the Inland Revenue Department, and for VAT once turnover crosses LKR 60 million a year.

Who you need

  • Directors: at least one. Foreign nationals can be directors; most advisors say no resident director is needed for a private company, but confirm with your corporate secretary.
  • Shareholders: 1 to 50 for a private company. No minimum capital, but at least one share must be issued.
  • Company secretary: must be a resident with a recognised qualification. Most foreign founders hire a corporate secretarial firm.

Ordinary company, BOI or Port City?

  • Ordinary registration: fine for most service businesses. Fastest and cheapest.
  • BOI approval: adds duty exemptions, work visas and room above sector caps. Section 16 approvals start at USD 250,000 of investment; Section 17 projects, with wider exemptions, are larger.
  • Port City Colombo: its own law and the 7.5% corporate tax or tax holidays for approved strategic businesses.

The India side: ODI

Investing in a foreign company from India runs under the FEMA Overseas Investment Rules and Regulations, 2022. An Indian company can commit up to 400% of its net worth, or USD 1 billion a year if lower, through its bank with Form FC and an Annual Performance Report each year. Individuals invest within the USD 250,000 LRS limit and cannot invest in financial services companies this way. Get your CA to structure it before money moves.

Already trading with Sri Lanka? You can invoice in rupees, and many goods cross duty free under the ISFTA.

Quick answers
Can an Indian own 100% of a company in Sri Lanka?

Yes, in most sectors. Some activities are capped at 40% unless the BOI approves more, including freight forwarding, shipping and travel agencies, education, and growing tea, spices and coconut. Retail trade needs at least USD 5 million of foreign capital.

How long does it take to register a company in Sri Lanka?

Usually 7 to 14 working days through the e-ROC portal: 3 to 5 days for name approval and 5 to 12 days for the certificate after filing.

Does a Sri Lankan company need a local director?

Most advisors say a private company needs at least one director, who can be a foreign national, with no resident director rule. The company secretary must be a qualified resident.

What is the corporate tax rate in Sri Lanka in 2026?

30% for most companies, with 45% for gaming, liquor and tobacco. Approved Port City businesses can get 7.5% for four years or a tax holiday.

What approvals does an Indian company need to invest in Sri Lanka?

On the Indian side, an Overseas Direct Investment filed through your bank under the FEMA Overseas Investment Rules 2022. On the Sri Lankan side, funds come in through an Inward Investment Account.

The Ramayana trail in Sri Lanka

For millions of Indian families, Lanka is not a destination. It is part of a story they grew up with. The island has a trail of sites associated by tradition with the Ramayana, and they fit neatly into a 7 to 10 day yatra.

A 7 day route

  1. Day 1: Arrive Colombo, drive to Chilaw for Munneswaram.
  2. Day 2: To Kandy, Temple of the Tooth and the hill road.
  3. Day 3: Nuwara Eliya: Sita Amman Temple, Hakgala, Divurumpola.
  4. Day 4: Ella: Ravana Falls and Ravana Cave.
  5. Day 5: South to Ussangoda and the coast.
  6. Day 6: Galle and Rumassala.
  7. Day 7: Back to Colombo and home.

Veg and Jain meals are easy to arrange along the route with notice. Large family groups usually travel by mini coach with a Hindi or Tamil speaking guide.

Quick answers
What are the Ramayana places in Sri Lanka?

Sites associated with the Ramayana include Sita Amman Temple, Hakgala (Ashok Vatika), Ravana Falls and Cave in Ella, Divurumpola, Munneswaram Temple, Ussangoda and Rumassala.

How many days do you need for the Ramayana tour in Sri Lanka?

Seven days covers the main sites at a comfortable pace. Ten days adds beaches or Yala and suits elderly travellers better.

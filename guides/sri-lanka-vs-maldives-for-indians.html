Sri Lanka vs Maldives for Indians: which one, and when

They are 90 minutes apart and often get compared, but they are different trips. The Maldives is one resort and a lagoon. Sri Lanka is hills, temples, safaris, a famous train and the beach, for a fraction of the nightly rate.

Who should pick what

  • Honeymoon on a sane budget: Sri Lanka. A week of hill country and a private beach villa costs less than three nights in a Maldives water villa. See our 7 night honeymoon plan.
  • Families and groups: Sri Lanka. More to do, cars with drivers, vegetarian food and temples.
  • Pure resort and water time: the Maldives, if the budget allows.
  • Both: fly Colombo to Malé and do five nights in Sri Lanka, then two in a Maldives resort.

Where Indians are actually going

5,31,511 Indians visited Sri Lanka in 2025, making India its biggest market by a distance. Indian arrivals to the Maldives fell from about 2,09,000 in 2023 to about 1,30,000 in 2024 after the diplomatic row, and were still below 2025 levels in mid 2026.

Quick answers
Is Sri Lanka cheaper than the Maldives for Indians?

Yes, usually by a wide margin. Mid range Sri Lankan hotels run about Rs 3,500 to 7,000 a night, while Maldives water villas cost around Rs 80,000 to 2 lakh or more a night per couple.

Do Indians need a visa for the Maldives or Sri Lanka?

Both are free for tourists. The Maldives gives a 30 day visa on arrival with a hotel booking and an online traveller declaration. Sri Lanka's 30 day ETA is free for Indians but must be approved online before you fly.

Which is better for a honeymoon, Sri Lanka or Maldives?

The Maldives for pure resort time. Sri Lanka for variety, hills, a train ride, wildlife and a beach villa finish, at a much lower cost. Many couples combine five nights in Sri Lanka with two in the Maldives.

Is there a direct flight from Chennai to the Maldives?

Not currently. Chennai has frequent direct flights to Colombo, about 1 hour 20 minutes, and Colombo connects to Malé.

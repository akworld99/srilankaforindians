New Year in Sri Lanka from India: where to be at midnight

Colombo puts on a proper New Year's Eve. To welcome 2026, fireworks went up over Galle Face Green, Port City and the Lotus Tower at the same time, and Galle Face filled with music and food stalls. It is 80 minutes from Chennai, the visa is free, and you are on the same clock as home.

A 4 night New Year plan from India

  1. 29 December: land in Colombo, drive down the expressway to Galle or Bentota.
  2. 30 December: Galle Fort, beach, whale watching from Mirissa if you are keen.
  3. 31 December: back to Colombo in the afternoon. Dinner, then Galle Face Green or a hotel gala for midnight.
  4. 1 January: a slow breakfast, Port City and Galle Face by day.
  5. 2 January: fly home.

Prefer the beach at midnight? Flip it: Colombo first, then a south coast resort or a surf town party for the 31st. See south coast parties and New Year galas.

Know before you go

  • Book now: this is the most expensive fortnight of the season, and it overlaps Indian school winter breaks. Flights and Colombo hotels with a Galle Face view sell out first.
  • Getting around on the 31st: police run a traffic plan around Galle Face, with about 1,200 officers deployed last year. Arrive early and walk or take a tuk tuk for the last stretch.
  • Poya and Christmas: 23 December is Unduvap Poya and 25 December is Christmas, both public holidays. Liquor shops close on Poya days; star hotels usually serve.
  • Visa and money: the tourist ETA is free for Indians, and UPI works at LankaQR merchants. Convert prices with our LKR to INR converter.

Sri Lanka's own New Year is in April

The island's biggest celebration is Sinhala and Tamil New Year, on 13 and 14 April 2027: family feasts, kiribath, games and an auspicious time to light the hearth. Closer to New Year's Day, Tamils celebrate Thai Pongal on 15 January 2027, and Duruthu Poya falls on 22 January, when the Kelaniya temple near Colombo holds its Duruthu Perahera procession.

Quick answers
Where can I celebrate New Year's Eve in Sri Lanka?

In Colombo, Galle Face Green is the big free public party with fireworks, and the city hotels run galas. On the south coast, Anantara Peace Haven Tangalle (USD 390 per adult), Amangalla and Cape Weligama have announced 2026 galas, and the surf towns of Hiriketiya, Ahangama and Mirissa run party nights.

Are there fireworks in Colombo on New Year's Eve?

Yes. To welcome 2026, fireworks went up over Galle Face Green, Port City and the Lotus Tower. Plans for 31 December 2026 are usually announced in November and December.

Is Sri Lanka expensive at New Year?

It is the peak of the season, so hotels and flights cost the most. Book by October or early November, and look at south coast villas shared between two families.

When is Sinhala and Tamil New Year in 2027?

13 and 14 April 2027. It is Sri Lanka's biggest festival, celebrated with family feasts, kiribath and traditional games.

Do Indians need a visa for a New Year trip to Sri Lanka?

An Electronic Travel Authorisation is required, but it is free for Indian tourists since 25 May 2026. Apply on eta.gov.lk before you fly.

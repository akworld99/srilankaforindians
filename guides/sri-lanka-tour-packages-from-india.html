Sri Lanka tour packages from India, 2026: what they cost

Five night Sri Lanka packages from big Indian operators start at about ₹44,000 per person in 2026, and Ramayana yatras run around ₹60,000. Here is what published prices look like, what to check before you pay, and when a custom trip wins.

What 5 to 7 nights should cost, flights included

  • Budget: ₹35,000 to 55,000 per person
  • Mid range: ₹60,000 to 90,000 per person
  • Luxury: ₹1 to 1.8 lakh per person

Line by line costs are in our trip cost guide.

Check these before you pay

  1. Flights: included, or land only? It is the biggest swing in the price.
  2. Car: private car with driver, or a shared coach?
  3. Meals: breakfast only, or all meals? Vegetarian or Jain on request?
  4. Entry tickets: Sigiriya, safaris and the train are often extra.
  5. The train: the Kandy to Ella line is only partly running in 2026. Ask how the package handles it.

Package or custom?

Packages win for first timers who want one price and a group. Custom wins for families of four or more, couples who want better hotels, and anyone with food needs. A car with a driver split four ways often beats per person package pricing. Tell us your city, dates and budget, and a vetted Sri Lankan operator quotes you in rupees.

Quick answers
How much is a Sri Lanka tour package from India?

Published 2026 prices start at about Rs 43,900 per person for five nights with major Indian operators, and around Rs 59,950 to 63,000 for six or seven day Ramayana yatras. Check whether flights are included.

Does the IRCTC Sri Lanka package include flights?

The 2026 IRCTC Ramayana Yatra from Bengaluru, priced from Rs 59,950 per person on triple sharing, includes SriLankan Airlines flights and all meals.

Is a package or a custom trip cheaper for Sri Lanka?

For families of four or more, a custom trip with a private car and driver often costs the same or less than package prices, with better hotels and food that suits you.

What is usually not included in Sri Lanka packages?

Often flights, entry tickets to sites like Sigiriya, safari jeeps, train tickets, tips and some meals. Ask for a written inclusions list.

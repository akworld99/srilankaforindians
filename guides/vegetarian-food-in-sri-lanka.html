Vegetarian and Jain food in Sri Lanka: what Indians need to know

Mostly yes. A Sri Lankan rice and curry spread usually has several vegetable curries, and Colombo has pure vegetarian South Indian restaurants that feel like home. The catch is one ingredient that most vegetarians never see coming.

The Maldive fish trap

Maldive fish is dried, cured tuna, and it goes into a lot of Sri Lankan cooking that looks vegetarian. It is essential in pol sambol, the coconut relish served with almost everything, and the main ingredient in seeni sambol, the sweet onion relish. Cooks also add it to vegetable curries for flavour. At any non vegetarian restaurant, ask clearly for food with no fish and no Maldive fish.

Pure vegetarian restaurants in Colombo

  • Shanmugas, Ramakrishna Road, Wellawatte: South and North Indian vegetarian.
  • Greenlands and Saraswathi, Wellawatte: long running vegetarian classics.
  • Sri Suryas, Sea Street: South Indian vegetarian.
  • Mathura and Shri Vihar: South Indian vegetarian.

Wellawatte, on the coast south of the city centre, has the biggest cluster of Tamil and Indian vegetarian kitchens. Stay nearby if food is a priority.

Outside Colombo

Indian travellers report that pure vegetarian food is easy to find in Colombo, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya and the Bentota area, and harder in Galle, Sigiriya and around Horton Plains. Hotels in those places will usually cook vegetable curries to order. Tell them a day ahead.

For Jain travellers

We have not found dedicated Jain restaurants in Sri Lanka. Families manage by telling their hotel in advance, asking South Indian vegetarian kitchens to leave out onion, garlic and root vegetables, and carrying ready to eat Jain meals for long drive days. A driver who knows your needs helps more than any app.

Vegetarian dishes worth trying

  • Dhal curry, jackfruit curry and beetroot curry at any rice and curry spread, once you have confirmed there is no Maldive fish.
  • Hoppers and string hoppers with a vegetarian curry for breakfast.
  • Kiribath, coconut milk rice, served at festivals and on New Year.

Sources: YAMU and Cleartrip lists of vegetarian restaurants in Colombo; Maldive fish references; Indian traveller reports. Restaurants change, so check opening hours before you go.

Quick answers
Is it easy to find vegetarian food in Sri Lanka?

Yes in Colombo, Kandy and the main tourist areas. Rice and curry spreads usually include several vegetable curries, and Colombo has pure vegetarian South Indian restaurants. Ask about Maldive fish, which is added to many sambols and some vegetable curries.

What is Maldive fish in Sri Lankan food?

Dried, cured tuna used as a flavouring. It is essential in pol sambol and the main ingredient in seeni sambol, and is often added to vegetable curries, so vegetarians should ask for food without it.

Where can I eat pure vegetarian food in Colombo?

Wellawatte has the biggest cluster, including Shanmugas, Greenlands and Saraswathi. Sri Suryas on Sea Street, Mathura and Shri Vihar also serve South Indian vegetarian food.

Is there Jain food in Sri Lanka?

Dedicated Jain restaurants are hard to find. Tell your hotel in advance, ask vegetarian kitchens to skip onion, garlic and root vegetables, and carry ready to eat meals for long drives.

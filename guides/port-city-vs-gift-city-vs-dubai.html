Headline rules only. Port City per Regulation 01 of 2025; GIFT City per Section 80LA of the Income Tax Act; UAE per Federal Decree Law 47 of 2022. Effective rates depend on structure.

The bit everyone forgets: residency

Zero personal tax only helps if you are not an Indian tax resident. Broadly, spending 182 days or more in India in a year makes you resident, and high earning Indian citizens face extra rules. Structure it with your CA before you move anyone.

Who should pick what

  • GIFT City: regulated financial services that want India's market with tax benefits and no relocation.
  • Dubai: global trading and holding structures, if Gulf exposure fits your risk appetite.
  • Port City: India plus one operations, GCCs, regional treasury and family offices that want a separate jurisdiction an hour from home.

Quick answers
Is Port City Colombo better than Dubai for Indian companies?

It depends on the business. Both offer zero personal income tax. Port City adds proximity to India and a different risk profile from the Gulf; Dubai offers a deeper, more established ecosystem.

What is the tax in GIFT City?

IFSC units in GIFT City can claim a 100% tax deduction for 10 out of 15 years under Section 80LA, while staying inside Indian jurisdiction.

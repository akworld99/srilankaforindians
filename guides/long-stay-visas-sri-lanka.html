Long stay visas in Sri Lanka for Indians, 2026

The free 30 day ETA is just the start. Indians who want a season, a year or a second base in Sri Lanka have four real routes in 2026. Here they are side by side.

RouteHow longWhat it takesFee
Tourist visa extensionUp to 270 days in totalApply inside Sri Lanka before the ETA expires, online or at BattaramullaUSD 60 to 200 by length
Digital nomad visa1 yearAge 18+, remote income of at least USD 2,000 a month from abroad, plus USD 500 a month per extra dependant beyond two. No local jobsUSD 500
Investor visa5 years, or 10 yearsUSD 100,000 for 5 years or USD 200,000 for 10, through an Inward Investment Account. A condo purchase qualifies. Spouse and dependants includedUSD 200 per person per year
Golden Paradise visa10 yearsUSD 200,000 deposited in a Sri Lankan bank; up to half can be withdrawn after the first yearUSD 2,000

The retiree "My Dream Home" visa is now open only to existing holders for extension. Fees shown are from Department of Immigration and Emigration pages and announcements; confirm current amounts when you apply.

Which one fits you

  • Testing island life: start with the free ETA and extend. Note that extending within the first 30 days cancels the second entry, and leaving the country ends an extension.
  • Working remotely for an Indian or global company: the digital nomad visa, launched in February 2026, is built for you. It does not allow working for a Sri Lankan employer.
  • Buying a home: the investor visa turns a USD 100,000 condo purchase into a 5 year base for the family. See the property rules for Indians.
  • Parking money, not buying: the Golden Paradise visa keeps funds on deposit instead.
  • Running a business here: directors and key staff of registered companies apply for employment or investor visas through the company.

Before you move

Staying abroad changes your Indian tax residency once you spend less than 182 days in India in a year, and that cuts both ways. Talk to your CA before you plan a full year away. For short trips, the tourist visa guide covers the free ETA and extensions step by step.

Quick answers
How can an Indian stay in Sri Lanka for more than 30 days?

Extend the free tourist ETA inside Sri Lanka, for up to 270 days in total, or apply for a longer visa: the digital nomad visa for one year, or the investor visa for 5 or 10 years.

Does Sri Lanka have a digital nomad visa?

Yes. It launched in February 2026. It runs for one year, costs USD 500, and needs remote income of at least USD 2,000 a month from outside Sri Lanka. Holders cannot work for local employers.

How much investment is needed for a Sri Lanka residence visa?

USD 100,000 for a 5 year investor visa or USD 200,000 for 10 years, routed through an Inward Investment Account. Buying a condominium counts.

Is the Sri Lanka retirement visa still available?

The My Dream Home retiree visa is now open only to existing holders for extensions. New long stay applicants usually look at the investor or digital nomad visas.

What is the Golden Paradise visa?

A 10 year residence visa for a USD 200,000 deposit in a Sri Lankan bank, with up to half withdrawable after the first year. The visa fee is USD 2,000.

Sri Lanka visa for Indians: free ETA, 2026

Since 25 May 2026, Indian passport holders pay nothing for Sri Lanka's 30 day tourist Electronic Travel Authorisation. India is one of 40 countries on the free list. You still have to apply online before you fly.

The short version

  • Cost: free for the tourist ETA (Indian passports, since 25 May 2026)
  • Stay: up to 30 days, double entry. Re entry uses the remaining days; the clock does not restart
  • Apply at: the official portal, eta.gov.lk. Ignore look alike agent sites that charge fees
  • Passport: valid at least six months from your date of entry
  • At the airport: officers may ask for a return ticket and proof of funds, and you fill a disembarkation card

How to apply

  1. Open eta.gov.lk and choose a tourist ETA.
  2. Enter passport details exactly as printed, travel dates and your first address in Sri Lanka.
  3. Submit. Approvals usually arrive by email within hours. Save the PDF on your phone.

Sri Lanka visa extension for Indians

The free 30 days can be extended, but the extension is paid. You apply inside Sri Lanka, before your current visa runs out.

  1. Where: online through the Department of Immigration and Emigration's tourist visa extension service, or in person at its head office in Battaramulla, Colombo. If the online application fails, go to the head office within 3 working days.
  2. What to carry: passport, a photo, your return ticket and the address where you are staying.
  3. How long: extensions run in bands up to a total of 270 days: 31 to 90 days, 91 to 180 days, and 181 to 270 days.
  4. Fees: charged in US dollars by band. The current fee table on immigration.gov.lk lists USD 60, 150 and 200 for the three bands for most nationalities. Confirm the figure for Indian passports when you apply.

Two rules that catch people out: applying for an extension during your first 30 days cancels the second entry on your ETA, and leaving Sri Lanka during an extension ends it. Overstaying costs USD 250 for 7 to 14 days and USD 500 beyond that.

Business travel

Meetings, conferences and site visits need a business ETA, not the free tourist one. For SAARC nationals, including Indians, it costs USD 30, allows multiple entry for 30 days, and is not issued on arrival, so apply before you fly.

Sources: Department of Immigration and Emigration notice on free tourist visas for 40 countries (effective 25 May 2026), visa extension and fee pages at immigration.gov.lk (October 2026). Always confirm on eta.gov.lk.

Quick answers
Is Sri Lanka visa free for Indians?

Indians still need an Electronic Travel Authorisation, but since 25 May 2026 the 30 day tourist ETA is free for Indian passport holders. Apply on eta.gov.lk before travel.

How long can Indians stay in Sri Lanka on the free ETA?

Up to 30 days with double entry. The 30 days do not restart if you leave and come back. Extensions can be applied for after arrival.

How do Indians extend a Sri Lanka visa?

Apply before your visa expires, online through the Department of Immigration and Emigration or at its Battaramulla head office, with your passport, a photo, a return ticket and your address. Extensions are paid and run up to a total of 270 days.

How much is a Sri Lanka visa extension?

Fees are charged in US dollars by length. The immigration fee table lists USD 60 for 31 to 90 days, USD 150 for up to 180 days and USD 200 for up to 270 days for most nationalities; confirm the Indian rate when you apply.

What is the Sri Lanka business visa fee for Indians?

A business ETA for SAARC nationals, including Indians, costs USD 30 and allows multiple entry for 30 days. It is not issued on arrival.

Sri Lanka itinerary from India: 5, 7 and 10 days

Flights from South India land in Colombo in under two hours, so day one starts by lunch. These three routes work for first timers, families and couples. Every drive time below is by car with a driver, the way most Indian families travel here.

5 days: Kandy and the south coast

DayPlanNight
1Land at Colombo, drive to Kandy (about 2.5 to 3 hours). Evening puja at the Temple of the ToothKandy
2Peradeniya botanical gardens, a tea factory, Kandy lake walkKandy
3Drive to Galle or Bentota (about 4 to 5 hours, the last stretch on the Southern Expressway). Sunset on the fort rampartsGalle
4Galle Fort, Unawatuna beach, turtle hatchery or a river safariGalle
5Colombo shopping on the way, fly home (Galle to the airport about 2 to 3 hours)Home

Quick answers
How many days are enough for Sri Lanka from India?

Seven days covers Kandy, tea country and a beach without rushing. Five days works for Kandy and Galle, and ten days adds Sigiriya and a wildlife safari.

Can I do Sri Lanka in 5 days?

Yes. From South India the flight is short, so five days comfortably covers Kandy and the Galle coast, with Colombo on the way back to the airport.

What is the best Sri Lanka route for first timers?

Colombo to Kandy, up to Nuwara Eliya and Ella in tea country, then down to Mirissa or Galle on the south coast.

Is the Kandy to Ella train running in 2026?

Partly. The Nanu Oya to Ella and Badulla section reopened on 20 June 2026, but sections between Kandy and Nanu Oya are still being repaired after Cyclone Ditwah, with full restoration targeted for the end of 2026. Drive to Nuwara Eliya and take the train from Nanu Oya.

How do Indian families usually get around Sri Lanka?

A car with a driver. It is the most comfortable option for families with luggage and splits well across four people.

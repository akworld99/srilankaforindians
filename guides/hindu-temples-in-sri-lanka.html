Hindu temples in Sri Lanka: the Pancha Ishwarams and beyond

Sri Lanka has some of the oldest Shiva shrines in the Tamil world. Five of them, the Pancha Ishwarams, ring the island's coast, and several are tied to the Ramayana. Here is where they are and how Indian pilgrims fit them into a trip.

The five Pancha Ishwarams of Shiva

TempleWhereWhy it matters
KoneswaramTrincomalee, east coastThe most visited of the five, on a cliff over the sea. Called "Then Kailayam" for its link to Mount Kailash
MunneswaramNear Chilaw, west coastWhere tradition says Rama sought atonement after defeating Ravana. An easy stop from Colombo airport
KetheeswaramManthai, MannarOne of the 275 Paadal Petra Sthalams praised in the Tevaram. Ravana is said to have worshipped here
NaguleswaramKeerimalai, near JaffnaLinked to the sage Nagula Muni, beside the Keerimalai springs
TondeeswaramDevinuwara (Dondra), far southThe southern shrine of the five, at the island's southern tip near Matara

More temples Indian families visit

  • Nallur Kandaswamy, Jaffna: Murugan's temple, with a 25 day festival in August and September that ends in a chariot procession. Sri Lanka's longest temple festival.
  • Kataragama, Uva Province: a shrine shared by Buddhists, Hindus and Muslims. Pilgrims walk the Pada Yatra from Jaffna for the Esala festival in July and August.
  • Seetha Amman, near Nuwara Eliya: about 8 km from town on the Badulla road, identified with Ashok Vatika. Marks on the rocks across the stream are revered as Hanuman's footprints. See the Ramayana trail.
  • Sri Ponnambalavaneswaram, Kotahena, Colombo: rebuilt in granite and consecrated in 1912, a rare all granite kovil.
  • New Kathiresan kovils, Colombo: the start and end of the Vel festival chariot procession each July or August between Pettah and Bambalapitiya.

How to fit them into a trip

  • From Colombo airport: Munneswaram is on the way north. Sri Ponnambalavaneswaram and the Kathiresan kovils are in the city.
  • North: fly from Chennai or Tiruchirappalli to Jaffna on IndiGo, or take the Nagapattinam ferry when it runs, for Nallur, Naguleswaram and Ketheeswaram.
  • East: pair Koneswaram with a Trincomalee beach stay from May to September, when the east coast is dry.
  • Hills and south: Seetha Amman on the tea country route, Kataragama with a Yala safari, Tondeeswaram on the south coast.

Quick answers
What are the Pancha Ishwarams in Sri Lanka?

Five ancient Shiva temples on the coast: Koneswaram in Trincomalee, Munneswaram near Chilaw, Ketheeswaram in Mannar, Naguleswaram at Keerimalai near Jaffna, and Tondeeswaram at Devinuwara in the south.

Which Hindu temple in Sri Lanka is linked to the Ramayana?

Several. Munneswaram is where Rama is said to have sought atonement, Ketheeswaram is linked to Ravana's worship, and Seetha Amman near Nuwara Eliya is identified with Ashok Vatika.

When is the Nallur festival in Jaffna?

The Nallur Kandaswamy festival runs for 25 days in August and September each year and ends with a chariot procession.

How do I reach Jaffna temples from India?

Fly direct to Jaffna from Chennai or Tiruchirappalli on IndiGo, or take the Nagapattinam to Kankesanthurai ferry when it is running. It pauses from late October or November until late February or March.

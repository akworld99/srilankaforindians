Is Sri Lanka safe for Indian tourists in 2026?

Yes, for normal tourist travel. Indians are Sri Lanka's biggest group of visitors: 4,31,162 arrived between January and September 2026, and India supplied about a third of all arrivals in September. Here is what the official advisories say and what actually matters on the ground.

What governments say

  • United States: Level 2 of 4, "exercise increased caution", the same level it gives many popular destinations.
  • Australia: "exercise a high degree of caution", its standard second level.
  • India: after Cyclone Ditwah in late 2025, the Indian High Commission in Colombo said key roads and routes were open and welcomed Indian tourists back.

The cyclone, and what it means for your trip

Cyclone Ditwah hit Sri Lanka in late 2025 and damaged parts of the hill country railway. Roads reopened first. The Nanu Oya to Ella train section reopened in June 2026, and the Colombo line now runs as far as Peradeniya, with full restoration of the Kandy routes targeted for the end of 2026. Plan to drive between Kandy and Nuwara Eliya and you lose nothing.

Practical safety tips

  • Getting around: use PickMe or Uber in Colombo, or agree a tuk tuk fare before you get in. Most families hire a car with a driver.
  • Monsoon: in heavy rain, avoid hill country hikes near steep slopes and follow local warnings.
  • The sea: swim at beaches with lifeguards or where locals swim. Currents are strong on the coast that is in monsoon.
  • Visa scams: apply only on eta.gov.lk. Look alike sites charge for a visa that is free for Indians.
  • Gem shops: buy certified stones from established jewellers, not from touts who approach you.
  • Money: UPI works at LankaQR merchants, so you carry less cash.

Check the latest advisories and the weather before you fly. Conditions change with the monsoon.

Quick answers
Is Sri Lanka safe for Indian tourists right now?

Yes, for normal tourist travel. Indians are Sri Lanka's largest visitor group in 2026. The US rates Sri Lanka at Level 2, exercise increased caution, the same as many popular destinations.

Is Sri Lanka safe for Indian families and solo women travellers?

Most Indian families and solo travellers report easy trips. Use registered taxis or a hired driver, keep to well used beaches, and follow the usual care you would take in any Indian tourist city.

Is it safe to travel to Sri Lanka after the cyclone?

Yes. Roads reopened soon after Cyclone Ditwah and the Indian High Commission welcomed Indian tourists back in December 2025. Some hill country rail sections remain under repair until the end of 2026.

Is the Kandy to Ella train running?

Partly. The Nanu Oya to Ella section reopened in June 2026; the sections from Kandy are being repaired, with full restoration targeted for the end of 2026.

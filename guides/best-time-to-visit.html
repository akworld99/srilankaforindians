Best time to visit Sri Lanka from India

Sri Lanka's cheat code: two monsoons hit different coasts at different times, so somewhere is always in season.

MonthsBest forNotes
December to AprilWest and south coasts, hill countryPeak season. Book weddings and villas early
May to SeptemberEast coast: Trincomalee, Pasikudah, Arugam BaySouth west monsoon on the other side; lower prices in the south
July or AugustKandy Esala PeraheraOne of Asia's great processions. Hotels fill fast
October to NovemberShoulder seasonInter monsoon showers, good deals, Deepavali trips

Indian school holidays line up nicely: summer break suits the east coast, and the Diwali to New Year window opens the south coast season.

Quick answers
What is the best time to visit Sri Lanka from India?

December to April for the west and south coasts and hill country; May to September for the east coast.

Can I visit Sri Lanka during the monsoon?

Yes. When one coast has monsoon rain, the other is usually dry, so plan your route around the season.

Paying in Sri Lanka

How Indians actually pay on the island.

  • UPI: scan LankaQR codes with your Indian UPI app at shops and restaurants that show them. Enable international UPI first.
  • Cards: Visa and Mastercard work in hotels, malls and most restaurants. A forex card avoids most markups.
  • Cash: take Sri Lankan rupees from an airport ATM for tuk tuks, temples and small shops.
  • Indian notes: you can carry up to Rs 25,000 out of India, but do not plan on spending them in Sri Lanka.

Doing business rather than travelling? Trade between Indian and Sri Lankan companies can be invoiced and settled in Indian rupees. See how rupee trade works.

Quick answers
What is 1 Sri Lankan rupee in Indian rupees?

About 0.29 Indian rupees. Put the other way, 1 Indian rupee buys about 3.49 Sri Lankan rupees (30 October 2026). The converter on this page loads the latest rate.

What is the quick way to convert LKR to INR in your head?

Divide the Sri Lankan price by 3.5. A LKR 3,500 dinner is about Rs 1,000, and a LKR 700 tuk tuk ride is about Rs 200.

Is the Indian rupee accepted in Sri Lanka?

Not for everyday payments. Shops, hotels and tuk tuks price in Sri Lankan rupees. The Indian rupee is on Sri Lanka's list of designated foreign currencies, which matters for banks and trade settlement, not for paying a shopkeeper.

Can I use UPI in Sri Lanka?

Yes, at merchants that display a LankaQR code. UPI went live in Sri Lanka in February 2024. Turn on international UPI in your app before you fly.

How much Indian cash can I carry out of India?

RBI allows Indian residents travelling abroad to carry up to Rs 25,000 in Indian currency notes, except to Nepal and Bhutan. For anything more, use a forex card, an international debit card or UPI.

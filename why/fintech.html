Questions

Asked by Indian founders and buyers.

Is Port City Colombo a financial centre?

Yes. It is a special economic zone with financial services as a core use, including the planned Colombo International Financial Centre, and it allows transactions in 16 designated foreign currencies.

Port City Colombo or GIFT City?

GIFT City keeps you inside Indian law with a 10 out of 15 year tax holiday for IFSC units. Port City adds a separate jurisdiction, full foreign ownership and zero personal income tax for approved firms' staff. See our full comparison guide.

Questions

Asked by Indian founders and buyers.

How many Indian tourists visit Sri Lanka?

431,162 Indians visited Sri Lanka between January and September 2026, making India the largest source market, according to the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority.

Can foreigners invest in hotels in Sri Lanka?

Yes, typically through long leases and Board of Investment approved structures. Hotels are one of the most common foreign real estate investments.

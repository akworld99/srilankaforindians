Why Sri Lanka for minerals

Some of the purest graphite on earth.

Sri Lanka is one of very few places mining vein graphite, a naturally high purity form dug from underground veins at Bogala and Kahatagaha. Mineral sands at Pulmoddai add ilmenite and rutile.

The India angle

What this means for Indian businesses.

India's battery and EV makers are hunting for diversified graphite supply. Sri Lankan vein graphite is a small but high quality option worth a technical evaluation.

Questions

Asked by Indian founders and buyers.

What is vein graphite?

A naturally occurring, highly crystalline graphite found in underground veins, notably in Sri Lanka, typically with very high natural carbon content.

Can Indian companies invest in Sri Lankan mining?

Mining requires licences from the Geological Survey and Mines Bureau and often approval through the Board of Investment. Partner with an experienced local operator.

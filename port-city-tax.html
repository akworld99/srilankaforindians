Port City Colombo · 2026 rules

7.5% corporate tax. Zero for the biggest bets.

Port City Colombo is a special economic zone with its own tax rulebook. Approved businesses can pay 7.5% corporate tax for their first four years, under a third of India's 25.17%. The largest projects, designated primary Businesses of Strategic Importance, pay nothing for 8 to 15 years, depending on how much they invest. Here is what changed in 2026, who qualifies, and what it is worth.

What changed in January 2026

The staff tax exemption is gone for new companies.

Until 2026, staff of approved Port City companies paid no personal income tax. The Amendment Act No. 1 of 2026 ended that for companies approved on or after 20 January 2026: their employees are taxed from day one. Companies approved before that keep the exemption for a three year transition, from 1 February 2026 to 31 January 2029. Corporate tax holidays for strategic investments remain, now with performance monitoring and reviews.

How to qualify

Getting the incentives in writing.

Size the project

Primary Businesses of Strategic Importance fall into four categories by investment and jobs, with holidays of 8, 10, 12 or 15 years. Below those thresholds, the secondary route offers 7.5% for four years.

Apply to the Commission

Straight answers
What is the tax rate in Port City Colombo in 2026?

Approved primary Businesses of Strategic Importance can receive corporate income tax holidays of 8 to 15 years after implementation, depending on investment size, under Regulation 01 of 2025. Secondary businesses can get a concessionary 7.5% corporate rate for 4 years. Other Port City companies pay normal Sri Lankan tax.

Is there still 0% personal income tax in Port City?

Not for new companies. The Colombo Port City Economic Commission (Amendment) Act No. 1 of 2026, certified on 20 January 2026, made employment income taxable from day one for companies approved on or after that date. Companies approved earlier keep a staff exemption for a 3 year transition, until 31 January 2029.

How big does an investment need to be for the 15 year tax holiday?

The longest holidays are reserved for the largest projects. Reporting in March 2026 described the full 15 years as applying to investments of USD 1 billion or more, with shorter holidays for lower tiers. Smaller approved businesses look at the secondary category: 7.5% for four years.

How does Port City compare with Dubai on tax?

The UAE charges 9% corporate tax above AED 375,000 and no personal income tax. Port City offers corporate tax holidays or a 7.5% rate to approved strategic businesses, but staff of newly approved companies now pay normal Sri Lankan income tax.

Can Port City incentives change again?

Yes. They were cut in 2023, restructured under Regulation 01 of 2025 and amended by Act No. 1 of 2026, which also introduced performance monitoring and reviews. Get your incentives written into your agreement before you invest.

Straight answers
What is the tax rate in Port City Colombo?

Under Regulation 01 of 2025, primary Businesses of Strategic Importance can receive corporate income tax holidays of 8 to 15 years depending on investment, secondary businesses a concessionary 7.5% corporate rate for 4 years, and staff of approved businesses have paid 0% personal income tax. All incentives require approval from the Colombo Port City Economic Commission.

Is Port City Colombo tax free for Indians?

The incentives apply to approved businesses in the zone regardless of the owner's nationality. Whether an individual Indian pays tax in India depends on Indian tax residency: residents are taxed on global income. Consult a chartered accountant.

How does Port City compare with Dubai on tax?

Both offer 0% personal income tax. The UAE charges 9% corporate tax above AED 375,000, with 0% possible on qualifying free zone income, while Port City offers multi year holidays or a 7.5% rate for approved businesses.

Can Port City incentives change?

Yes. Incentives were revised in 2023 and again under Regulation 01 of 2025, and commentators have flagged possible changes to the personal tax exemption. Lock in terms in writing with the Commission before you invest.

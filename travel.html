The practicals Indians actually ask

Visa

Apply at the official eta.gov.lk. Approvals usually land within hours. Sri Lanka has run free visa schemes for Indians; check the fee at application time. Carry the ETA printout or PDF.

Money and UPI

UPI works at LankaQR merchants in cities and tourist areas. Carry some LKR cash for small vendors. Cards work in hotels and supermarkets.

Getting around

PickMe is the local Ola. Intercity: hire a car with driver for the week, it costs less than you expect and the driver becomes your fixer. The hill train is an experience, book reserved seats early.

Best season

West and south coasts shine December to April. East coast (Trinco, Arugam Bay) takes over May to September. There is always a good coast; that is the island's cheat code.

Safety

Tourist areas are relaxed and used to Indian visitors. Normal travel sense applies. Solo women travellers rate it above most of South Asia.
